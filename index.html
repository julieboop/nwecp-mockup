Vision Problems After a Brain Injury

Many people who have had a concussion or stroke are told their eyes are healthy and their vision is 20/20, yet they still have trouble reading, working on a computer, driving or being in busy places like a grocery store. In these cases the problem is usually not in the eye itself. It is in how the brain processes the information the eyes send it.

Common symptoms include:

  • Headaches or fatigue with reading or screen use
  • Dizziness, nausea or a feeling of being off balance
  • Sensitivity to light, especially fluorescent lighting
  • Double vision, or words that appear to move on the page
  • Losing your place while reading
  • Difficulty in crowded or visually busy environments

These problems can be measured and, in most cases, treated. A neuro-optometric evaluation takes one to two hours and looks at eye movement, focusing, eye teaming, visual processing and how vision interacts with balance. If you have any of these symptoms, please call one of our offices to schedule an evaluation.

Information for New Patients

Before your appointment

After you schedule, we will send a link by email or text to complete your patient history and intake forms online. Completing these ahead of time shortens your check-in. Please bring your insurance card, a photo ID, your current glasses and contact lenses, and a list of your medications.

What to expect

A routine eye exam usually takes about an hour. A neuro-optometric evaluation takes one to two hours, because it includes a longer set of tests than a standard exam. A technician will take preliminary measurements before you see the doctor. Our doctors review your history before your visit so that your time in the exam room can be spent on your questions and your treatment plan.

Insurance and payment

We accept most major medical and vision insurance plans. Because neuro-optometric care is often billed to medical rather than vision insurance, please call the office ahead of time if you have questions about your coverage. Payment is due at the time of service. You can also pay your bill online through the patient portal.

Patient portal

The patient portal lets you complete forms, view statements, pay your balance and access home exercise materials assigned by your therapist.

For Referring Providers

We work with neurologists, physiatrists, occupational and physical therapists, speech-language pathologists and primary care providers throughout Oregon and Southwest Washington. If you have a patient with visual complaints following a concussion, stroke or other neurological event, we are happy to evaluate them and coordinate care with your office.
