Vision Problems After a Concussion or Stroke

Many patients are told their eyes are healthy and their vision is 20/20 after a head injury or stroke, but they still have trouble with reading, screens, driving or crowded places. The problem is often in how the brain processes what the eyes see, which a standard eye exam does not test for. Symptoms we commonly see include:

  • Headaches or fatigue with reading or screen use
  • Dizziness, nausea or feeling off balance
  • Sensitivity to light
  • Double vision, or words that seem to move on the page
  • Difficulty in busy places such as stores or traffic

A neuro-optometric evaluation takes one to two hours and can identify these problems so they can be treated. If you have any of these symptoms, please call one of our offices.

Conditions We Treat

  • Concussion and traumatic brain injury
  • Stroke and visual field loss
  • Convergence insufficiency
  • Strabismus (eye turn)
  • Amblyopia (lazy eye)
  • Learning-related vision problems
  • Glaucoma, macular degeneration and diabetic eye disease
  • Cataract co-management
