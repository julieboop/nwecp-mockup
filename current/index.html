Welcome to Northwest Eye Care Professionals!

Serving Clackamas and the surrounding communities in Vancouver, we offer comprehensive eye health services for all members of your family as well as specialty services. We evaluate and treat those that have difficulty with learning due to visual processing and rehabilitative care for incidents such as stroke or brain injury. We know how much your eye health and appearance means to the quality of your life. Our Doctors and staff are committed to excellence and serving your complete eye care needs.

Visit us at Northwest Eye Care Professionals to find the latest in fashion eyewear and lens technology. Our experienced staff looks forward to helping you find the best pair of eyewear or contact lenses to meet your visual needs.

If you are new to our website, we invite you to explore and discover all there is to learn about your vision.

We look forward to welcoming you into our office at Northwest Eye Care Professionals soon!
